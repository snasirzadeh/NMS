# Phase 03: Groups And Devices

## Contract

Implement hierarchical groups and device inventory with ownership validation,
Alembic migrations, management IP/SSH port validation, device metadata, group
tree UI, inventory UI, and add-device workflow. Do not make real SSH
connections; parsing belongs to the next contract.

## Outcome

The original company terminology was replaced with hierarchical Groups,
including nested inventory ownership and device CRUD APIs/UI.
